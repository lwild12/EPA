# UK legal and regulatory reference (checked 8 November 2026 - verify before citing)

| Topic | Current position | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Data protection law | UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018, amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 | Principles, lawful basis, DPIAs, breach reporting (72 h) |
| Data protection regulator | **Information Commission**, which succeeded the ICO on **30 September 2026** | Source: GOV.UK announcement. Older material still says ICO; use the new name, and mention the change once |
| Computer misuse | Computer Misuse Act 1990 (s1 unauthorised access, s2 with intent, s3 impairment, s3A articles for use) | Why you only test on your own lab / authorised systems |
| Network and information systems | NIS Regulations 2018 | Operators of essential services and digital service providers |
| New cyber legislation | **Cyber Security and Resilience Bill** | Parliament's bill page (last updated 12 June 2026) showed it at Commons Report Stage and **not yet** Royal Assent. Re-check https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/4035 before stating its status |
| Fraud and cyber crime reporting | **Report Fraud** (City of London Police service) replaced Action Fraud, with full public launch in January 2026 | NCSC for cyber incident reporting |
| Surveillance and monitoring | Investigatory Powers Act 2016; Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000; Human Rights Act (Art. 8) | Staff monitoring must be lawful, necessary, proportionate and notified |
| Evidence | Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984; ACPO/NPCC digital evidence principles | Chain of custody, no alteration of original |
| Copyright and licensing | Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 | Cite third-party tools and code |
| Security standards (not law) | Cyber Essentials, ISO/IEC 27001, NCSC CAF | Often contractually required |

## Pointers for the write-up
- Name the specific duty a control supports (e.g. "encryption at rest supports UK GDPR Art. 32 security of processing").
- Keep law claims short and sourced; markers reward correct application more than long lists.
- Re-verify anything marked above if you are writing after the check date.
